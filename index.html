More than a team

Built for the next play.

The Yuba Ravens are a competitive girls fastpitch travel softball organization serving the surrounding Yuba-Sutter area. We create a positive environment for athletes who want to compete, improve, and grow together.

01

Development

Purposeful practices that build fundamentals, confidence, softball IQ, and competitive habits.

02

Team Culture

Work hard, support teammates, respect the game, and represent the Ravens with pride.

03

Local Roots

Practices and team activities centered in the Yuba City and Olivehurst community.

Season information

2026 Fall / Winter 10U

Focused training, quality competition, and a supportive team experience for developing athletes.

◆

Timeline

August start, tournaments September–November, and winter training December–January.

◆

Practices

Two practices per week, typically 2.5–3 hours, in the Yuba City and Olivehurst area.

◆

Tournaments

Four to five Legacy and AST events, plus local round robins when available.

◆

Initial Cost

$250 deposit at the first meeting, covering uniforms, helmets, and insurance.
