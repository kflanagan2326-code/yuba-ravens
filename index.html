About the Program

The Yuba Ravens are a competitive girls fastpitch travel softball organization focused on player development, teamwork, accountability, and creating a positive environment for athletes who want to compete and grow.

Player Development

Focused practices designed to build softball fundamentals, confidence, softball IQ, and competitive habits.

Team Culture

Players are expected to work hard, support teammates, respect the game, and represent the Ravens with pride.

Local Roots

Practices and team activities are based around the Yuba City and Olivehurst area.

2026 Fall / Winter 10U Information

Season Timeline

Approximate start in August, tournaments from September through November, and winter training from December through January.

Practices

Two practices per week, typically 2.5 to 3 hours each, in the Yuba City and Olivehurst area.

Tournaments

Expected events include 4 to 5 Legacy and AST tournaments, plus local round robins when available.

Initial Cost

$250 initial deposit due at the first meeting. This covers uniforms, helmets, and insurance.

Tryouts

The Ravens look for dedicated softball players who are coachable, hardworking, team-focused, and ready to compete. Tryout details, dates, and paperwork information can be posted here as they are announced.

  • Player name, birth year, and primary positions
  • Parent/guardian contact information
  • Medical and emergency contact information
  • Softball experience and availability

Fundraising & Sponsorships

Fundraising opportunities and community sponsorships help reduce overall season expenses for players and families. As a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, the Ravens can accept sponsorship support and tax-deductible donations.
